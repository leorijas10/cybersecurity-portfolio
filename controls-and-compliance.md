## Key Security Control Gaps

### Least Privilege

Employees should only have access to the information and systems necessary for their work.

Implementing least privilege can reduce the risk of unauthorized access and limit the potential impact of a compromised account.

### Password Security

Strong password policies and appropriate password management can reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

The organization should establish stronger password requirements and consider a secure password management system.

### Encryption

Sensitive information should be protected using encryption to reduce the risk of unauthorized disclosure.

### Backups and Disaster Recovery

Regular backups and a disaster recovery plan can help the organization recover from data loss and other disruptions.

### Intrusion Detection

An intrusion detection system could help the organization identify suspicious network activity and improve its ability to detect potential security incidents.

### Legacy Systems

Legacy systems can create additional security risks if they are outdated or difficult to maintain.

The organization should monitor these systems and address security weaknesses where possible.

## Compliance Considerations

### PCI DSS

The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) is relevant to organizations that handle payment card information.

Important security considerations include:

- Protecting payment card data
- Restricting access to sensitive information
- Maintaining secure systems and networks
- Monitoring access to payment-related information

### GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provides requirements for protecting personal data when it applies to an organization.

Important considerations include:

- Protecting personal information
- Limiting access to personal data
- Maintaining appropriate security measures
- Protecting individuals' privacy rights

### SOC

SOC reports are used to evaluate an organization's controls.

SOC 1 focuses on controls related to financial reporting, while SOC 2 focuses on controls related to areas such as security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

## Recommended Priorities

Based on the assessment, the organization should prioritize:

1. Improving access controls and least privilege.
2. Strengthening password security.
3. Protecting sensitive data with encryption.
4. Establishing reliable backups.
5. Developing a disaster recovery plan.
6. Improving detection of suspicious activity.
7. Monitoring and addressing risks associated with legacy systems.

---

## Conclusion

The assessment identified several security control gaps that could increase the organization's risk.

Improving access control, password security, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, and security monitoring can help strengthen the organization's overall security posture.
